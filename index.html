1 知己知彼：口說遊戲規則

考試流程 (11-14分鐘)

  • •
    Part 1 日常問答 (4-5分)： 熱身題，像認識新朋友的閒聊。重點是自然流暢。
  • •
    Part 2 個人獨白 (3-4分)： 拿題卡，準備1分鐘，連續講1-2分鐘。考驗敘事能力。
  • •
    Part 3 深度討論 (4-5分)： 根據 Part 2 延伸的抽象社會議題。考驗邏輯與批判思考。

四大評分標準 (各佔25%)

  • •
    FC (流暢度與連貫性)： 不卡頓，會使用連接詞 (Furthermore, Therefore)。
  • •
    LR (詞彙多樣性)： 不一直重複同個字，會用片語/慣用語 (idioms)。
  • •
    GRA (文法準確性)： 時態正確(過去的故事用過去式！)，會用複合句。
  • •
    PR (發音)： 不求腔調像外國人，但求語調有起伏、重音發對。

6 萬用高分句型模組 (情境分類大全)

不要再從頭到尾只會說 "I think", "And", "Because" 了！將以下句型依據情境替換，能大幅提升 GRA (文法多樣性) 的分數。

🗣️ 表達強烈觀點 (取代 I think)
  • • Personally, I strongly believe that...
  • • As far as I'm concerned,...
  • • To my mind,...
💡 例：
To my mind, this policy will benefit everyone in the long run.
⚖️ 對比與讓步 (Part 3必備)
  • • On the one hand... On the other hand...
  • • While some argue that..., I tend to think...
  • • Granted, [A], but we shouldn't ignore [B]...
💡 例：
Granted, technology brings convenience, but we shouldn't ignore its impact on privacy.
➕ 補充延伸細節 (取代 And / Also)
  • • On top of that,...
  • • What's more,... / Additionally,...
  • • Another crucial point to consider is...
💡 例：
Another crucial point to consider is the environmental cost of this project.
🎯 舉例與具體化 (取代 For example)
  • • A prime example of this would be...
  • • Take [事物] as a perfect case in point.
  • • To give you a clearer picture,...
💡 例：
Take online learning as a perfect case in point; it offers immense flexibility.
⏱️ 爭取思考時間 (不著痕跡的拖延)
  • • That's a really intriguing question.
  • • Off the top of my head, I'd say...
  • • It's hard to say for certain, but...
💡 例：
I've never thought about it, but off the top of my head, I'd say yes.
🎬 完美總結收尾 (Part 2 & 3 適用)
  • • So, all things considered,...
  • • Ultimately, it all comes down to...
  • • That's basically why I feel this way.
💡 例：
Ultimately, it all comes down to how much funding the school receives.

7 單字大升級 (12組高頻互動字卡)

✨ 滑鼠移過去 / 點擊 看看怎麼替換成高級單字，並熟記背面的短例句，直接提升 LR (詞彙多樣性) 分數！

Stop using very good
phenomenal
marvelous Ex: The view was phenomenal.
Stop using very bad
detrimental
appalling Ex: It has a detrimental effect.
Stop using many / a lot
a multitude of
a vast array of Ex: We face a multitude of issues.
Stop using important
crucial
indispensable Ex: Phones are indispensable now.
Stop using I like...
passionate about
fond of Ex: I'm passionate about coding.
Stop using beautiful (形容風景)
breathtaking
picturesque Ex: The scenery was breathtaking.
Stop using happy
thrilled
on cloud nine Ex: I was on cloud nine when I passed.
Stop using famous
renowned
well-known Ex: He is renowned for his art.
Stop using change
transform
revolutionize Ex: AI will revolutionize the industry.
Stop using crowded
packed
heavily congested Ex: The downtown is heavily congested.
Stop using boring
monotonous
tedious Ex: Filing documents is a tedious task.
Stop using useful
practical
highly beneficial Ex: Learning coding is highly beneficial.
