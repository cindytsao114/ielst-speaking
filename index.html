1 知己知彼：口說遊戲規則

⏱️ 考試流程 (11-14分鐘)

  • 🔹
    Part 1 日常問答 (4-5分)： 熱身題，像認識新朋友的閒聊。重點是自然流暢。
  • 🔹
    Part 2 個人獨白 (3-4分)： 拿題卡，準備1分鐘，連續講1-2分鐘。考驗敘事能力。
  • 🔹
    Part 3 深度討論 (4-5分)： 根據 Part 2 延伸的抽象社會議題。考驗邏輯與批判思考。

🎯 四大評分標準 (各佔25%)

  • 💡
    FC (流暢度與連貫性)： 不卡頓，會使用連接詞 (Furthermore, Therefore)。
  • 💡
    LR (詞彙多樣性)： 不一直重複同個字，會用片語/慣用語 (idioms)。
  • 💡
    GRA (文法準確性)： 時態正確(過去的故事用過去式！)，會用複合句。
  • 💡
    PR (發音)： 不求腔調像外國人，但求語調有起伏、重音發對。

2 Part 1：生活暖身題破解 & 實戰範例

目標： 展現自然流暢。不要只回答 Yes/No，一定要用 「直接回答 + 2句話延伸 (原因/細節/例子)」 的公式。

情境一：Hometown (家鄉)

🗣️ Q: Has your hometown changed much in recent years?

❌ 弱回答: Yes, it has changed a lot. There are many new buildings. (太簡短)

✅ 高分回答: Definitely! Over the past decade, my hometown has undergone massive changes. (細節) For instance, the local government has built a new subway system, (結果) which makes commuting so much easier than before.

💡 點評：用 definitely 取代 yes，並給出了「地鐵」這個具體細節，時態也正確使用了現在完成式。

情境二：Free Time (休閒時間)

🗣️ Q: What do you usually do on weekends?

✅ 高分回答: Well, it really depends. If I have a heavy workload, I might just stay home and catch up on some reading. (對比) However, if the weather is nice and I'm free, I absolutely love going hiking with my friends to get away from the hustle and bustle of the city.

💡 點評：用語 "It really depends" 非常道地。使用 "catch up on" (補進度), "hustle and bustle" (喧囂) 等片語大加分！

3 Part 2：魔王獨白題破解 & 實戰範例

目標： 故事完整、時態正確。善用黃金 1 分鐘準備時間，只寫關鍵字，使用 5W1H 架構 來豐富內容。

📝 模擬考題 (Cue Card)

Describe a skill you learned that you think is very useful.
- What the skill is
- When and how you learned it
- Who you learned it from
- And explain why you think it is useful

✍️ 過來人的 1 分鐘筆記 (切忌寫完整句子)
  • - What: Cooking (pasta, steak)
  • - When: Last year, pandemic
  • - How/Who: YouTube, trial & error
  • - Why: Save $, healthier, stress-relief
🎙️ 滿分作答示範 (節錄)

"I'd like to talk about cooking, which is undoubtedly one of the most practical skills I've ever acquired.

I actually picked up this skill during the lock-down last year. Since all restaurants were closed, I had no choice but to learn it myself. Instead of taking a formal class, I basically learned everything from watching YouTube...

The main reason why I find it so useful is that it's not only incredibly cost-effective but also much healthier..."

4 Part 3：抽象學術題破解 (OREO邏輯法)

目標： 展現邏輯與批判思考。考官問的不再是「你」，而是「社會現象」。請套用 OREO 法則 擴充答案：Opinion (觀點) -> Reason (原因) -> Example (舉例) -> Outcome (結論)。

情境：Technology & Society (科技與社會)

🗣️ Q: Do you think AI will eventually replace human teachers?

O

Opinion: To be honest, I don't entirely agree with that idea. (老實說，我不完全同意。)

R

Reason: This is mainly because education is also about emotional connection, which AI lacks. (因為教育包含情感連結)

E

Example: For instance, when a student is discouraged, a human teacher can offer empathy, whereas a machine only analyzes data.

O

Outcome: Therefore, AI will be an assistant, but never replace the human touch.

5 考場求生：遇到困難怎麼辦？

🤔

沒聽懂題目

有禮貌地請考官重複或解釋是完全不扣分的！

"Sorry, could you rephrase the question, please?"
⏳

需要時間思考

絕對不要發出漫長的 "Ummm"，用自然的句子把思考時間合理化。

"That's a really tricky question. Let me think..."
🙊

講錯文法

不要驚慌道歉，自然地自我糾正反而有助於分數。

"He go... I mean, he went to the store."

6 萬用高分句型模組

  • 表達強烈觀點 (取代 I think) 💡 Personally, I strongly believe that... 💡 As far as I'm concerned,...
  • 對比兩面看法 (Part 3必備) 💡 On the one hand... On the other hand... 💡 While some people argue that... I tend to think...
  • 補充說明 (取代 And) 💡 On top of that,... 💡 What's more,... / Additionally,...

7 單字大升級 (互動字卡)

💡 滑鼠移過去 / 點擊 看看怎麼替換成高級單字：

very good
phenomenal / marvelous ✨
very bad
detrimental / awful 🚫
many / a lot of
a multitude of / a vast array of 📚
important
crucial / vital / indispensable 🔑
I like...
I'm passionate about... ❤️
